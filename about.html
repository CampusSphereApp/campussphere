About CampusSphere

CampusSphere is being built to bring students, communities, events, projects, opportunities and meaningful campus connections into one unified platform.

Built by students, for the way campus life actually works.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING

Our Mission

Campus life is filled with opportunities, people and communities, but discovering and connecting with them is often fragmented. CampusSphere aims to create one digital ecosystem where students can discover, connect, collaborate, participate and grow.
