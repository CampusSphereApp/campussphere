Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is CampusSphere?

A digital campus ecosystem connecting people, social activity, projects, communities, events, opportunities and academic workflows.

Who can use CampusSphere?

CampusSphere is designed for students, teachers and college teams. Features depend on a person’s verified role.

Is CampusSphere only for students?

No. Teachers and authorized college teams have role-aware tools for their work.

Can teachers use CampusSphere?

Yes. Teacher workflows include classes, subjects, timetables, students and attendance.

Can colleges use CampusSphere?

College administration flows exist; access and onboarding depend on institution setup during beta.

Can students from different colleges connect?

The product is designed as a wider campus network, while discovery may use college context and verification.

Is CampusSphere free for students?

CampusSphere is in beta. Any future pricing will be communicated clearly before it applies.

How does college verification work?

Identity and roles can be tied to registration and invitation flows. Availability may vary by participating college.

Is CampusSphere currently in beta?

Yes. CampusSphere is actively being developed and improved as a beta product.
